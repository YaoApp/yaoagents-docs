# Kanban Board + Inbox — Queue It Up, AI Knocks It Down

![Kanban Board](https://assets.yaoagents.com/en/kanban/07-kanban-board2-en-$__THEME.png)

Chatting with AI one task at a time works fine — until you've got seven things in flight. Switching between conversations gets old fast.

**Kanban Board** puts everything on one surface. Create a board, set up columns, toss in tasks. AI picks them up and runs. Drag cards around to reprioritize whenever you want.

**Inbox** watches your tasks so you don't have to. Done? You'll know. Stuck on a question? It asks. You reply, it keeps going.

## Kanban Board: line up the work, let AI run it

**Type it, ship it** — Hit "+", say what you need in plain language. The second you send that message, AI is on it.

![Create a task](https://assets.yaoagents.com/en/kanban/03-kanban-newtask-en-$__THEME.png)

**Everything on one board** — "This Week," "Recurring," "On Hold," "Done" — make whatever columns fit your workflow. Drag to reorder.

**Set it and forget it** — Weekly report every Monday? Brand monitoring every 24 hours? Schedule it once. Recurring tasks keep their conversation history, so AI builds on what it did last time.

**AI checks in when it's unsure** — Hit a fork in the road? AI pauses and waits for your call instead of guessing.

## Inbox: your task feed

![Inbox](https://assets.yaoagents.com/en/inbox/01-inbox-home-en-$__THEME.png)

**Three signals**: needs your input (top priority), task completed, task failed. Filter by type to zero in on what matters.

**Reply in place** — AI asks "plan A or plan B?" — answer right there in Inbox. The task picks back up. No hunting for the right chat window.

![Reply to AI](https://assets.yaoagents.com/en/inbox/03-inbox-detail2-en-$__THEME.png)

**Star and pin** — Flag what's important, pin what's urgent.

## What this looks like in practice

You run marketing for a coffee brand. Here's your board:

| Column | Tasks |
|--------|-------|
| This Week | Cold brew guide, July newsletter draft, competitor social audit |
| Recurring | Instagram weekly report (Mon 9 AM), daily brand mention scan |
| On Hold | Summer shoot creative brief (waiting on photographer) |
| Done | Coffee bloom science article |

Seven tasks, one board. AI crunches through them in the background. You do your thing.

Midway through the cold brew guide, AI asks: "Open with the science or tell a story?" Inbox pops up. You say "story." It keeps writing.

Competitor audit wraps up? Inbox lets you know. Click through to the report.

# Android Beta Is Live — Command Your Computers From Your Phone

![Command your computers from your phone](https://assets.yaoagents.com/en/blog/blog-cover-phone-command-cluster-16x9.png)

Android (Beta) is here — grab it from the [download page](/download/yaoagents). Your AI experts run on your own computers — now you can hand out work and check in on it from your phone.

> This Android app was itself built with Yao Agents. The first version that actually ran was built on the desktop client, and it kept getting refined on the phone from there. Most of those changes happened while away from the computer — out for a walk or shopping, pulling out the phone and telling the AI on the machines back at the office to keep working.

## What you can do on your phone

### Kanban — check on progress from anywhere

Your AI experts are working on your machines. Open the board on your phone, and you can see at a glance what's done and what's stuck.

![Kanban board](https://assets.yaoagents.com/en/mobile/kanban-en-$__THEME.png)

### Experts — pick who you want to talk to

Every expert is on your phone too. Tap one and start chatting.

![AI Experts](https://assets.yaoagents.com/en/mobile/experts-en-$__THEME.png)

### New task — hand work off on the go

Choose your workspace, expert, and model, then just hold to speak. You can hand things off while you're out walking, waiting in line, or at the store.

![New task](https://assets.yaoagents.com/en/mobile/new-task-en-$__THEME.png)

## How to install

Android is in Beta for now. Get it from the [download page](/download/yaoagents), then sign in to the same workspaces as your desktop app.

# Account Migration: Cloud Service → Tao Service

Starting **September 23, 2026 at 15:00 UTC**, we'll migrate accounts from Cloud Service to Tao Service and turn on the new account system.

- **Balance**: your Cloud Service balance converts to Tao Service credits of the same value.
- **Sign-up bonus**: everyone who registered before the migration starts will get their sign-up bonus credits.
- **Downtime**: the migration should take about 2 hours, and you won't be able to log in during that time.

Nothing you need to do — once it's done, just sign in with your existing account.

Questions? Email us at [support@yaoagents.com](mailto:support@yaoagents.com).

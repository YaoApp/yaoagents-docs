# Connect WeChat

Connect your robot to WeChat so users can chat with it directly in the app — no extra software required on their end.

WeChat integration works through the **ClawBot plugin**, an official Tencent channel plugin built into WeChat's plugin system. Once connected, the robot appears as a regular WeChat contact, and any user who adds it as a friend can start chatting immediately.

## Before You Start

- Your robot must already be created in Mission Control.
- Prepare a dedicated WeChat account for the robot (a personal account is fine — no official account or mini-program needed).
- Keep WeChat updated to the latest version on your phone (iOS and Android are both supported).
- The Yao Agents server must stay online both during the scan and while the robot is running.

## Step 1 — Open the Integration Panel

1. In Mission Control, click the robot you want to connect.
2. Go to the **Config → Integration** tab.
3. Find the **WeChat** card and enable the toggle.

![Mission Control — Integration panel, WeChat card](https://placehold.co/1200x750/1a1a2e/ffffff?text=Screenshot+needed%3A+Robot+Integration+panel+%E2%80%94+WeChat+card)

## Step 2 — Scan to Connect

Click **Scan to Connect** to open the QR code dialog in Mission Control.

On the phone with the robot's WeChat account, open the ClawBot plugin:

**Me → Settings → Plugins → WeChat ClawBot → Details**

Inside the ClawBot plugin detail page, tap **Start Scan** and scan the QR code displayed in Mission Control.

> 💡 The QR code expires in 3 minutes. Close the dialog and reopen it to refresh.

**On first connection**, WeChat will ask you to authorize the ClawBot plugin for this account. Confirm the authorization to complete the connection.

After a successful scan, the dialog closes automatically and the card shows **Connected** with the WeChat account name.

## Step 3 — Save

Click **Save** (the save button at the top of the config panel). The robot is now live on WeChat.

Send a message to the WeChat account from any phone and the robot will reply.

## Reconnecting

If the connection drops (e.g. after a server restart), open the Integration panel and click **Reconnect** on the WeChat card to scan again.

## Troubleshooting

**"ClawBot plugin not installed" or "Feature not supported."**
Your WeChat version is outdated. Update WeChat to the latest version and try again.

**The "Connected" status never appears after scanning.**
Make sure you are using WeChat iOS 8.0.70+ and have authorized the ClawBot plugin. Also confirm the account is not simultaneously logged in on another device.

**The robot stops responding after a while.**
The session may have timed out. Reconnect by scanning the QR code again. Keeping the Yao Agents server running continuously prevents interruptions.

**"Already logged in on another device."**
Sign out of that WeChat account on the other device first, then scan again.
